About FLOODLENS & Hydrological Methodology

Transparent scientific framework, open APIs, and mathematical attribution formulas.

Project Mission

FLOODLENS is an open-source, satellite-backed flood monitoring and risk intelligence system designed for disaster management authorities, municipal emergency planners, and citizens in flood-vulnerable coastal and riverine settlements. By synthesizing NASA GIBS Earth observations, Copernicus GloFAS river routing, and high-frequency meteorological telemetry, FLOODLENS bridges the critical gap between raw satellite telemetry and explainable community risk mitigation.

Severity Classification Tiers

LOW RISK (0 - 24) Normal

Stable drainage conditions; minor ponding in standard street dips; routine civic functioning.

MODERATE RISK (25 - 49) Advisory

Waterlogging across underpasses, service lanes, and poorly drained lowlands. Cautious vehicular traffic.

HIGH RISK (50 - 74) Warning

Widespread street inundation, overflowing stormwater canals, ground-floor water entry in low elevations.

SEVERE RISK (75 - 100) Emergency

Catastrophic flood hazard; extreme torrential downpour, river out-of-bank breach, emergency evacuation protocol.

Scientific Disclaimer & Hackathon Transparency Notice

FLOODLENS is an experimental hackathon software application designed to demonstrate multi-source disaster data fusion. It is NOT certified as an official sole-source operational early-warning platform by governmental civil defense bodies.

Ordinary optical satellite imagery reflects visible and infrared surface reflectance; it does NOT penetrate dense cloud cover during active storm systems and CANNOT alone confirm ground-truth flooding without radar (SAR) or field calibration. Crowdsourced reports are explicitly labeled as "Unverified" until corroborated by authorized emergency responders.
