GloFAS Hydrodynamic Modeling

The Global Flood Awareness System (GloFAS) is co-developed by the European Commission and the European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF). It routes runoff from numerical weather prediction forecasts through a global river network model with a 0.1° (~10 km) grid resolution.

When an anomaly ratio exceeds 1.5x to 2.0x seasonal normal, rivers approach bankfull stage and pose serious overbank flood hazards.

Zero Fake Data Integrity Standard

FLOODLENS adheres strictly to scientific data integrity. When a coordinate is located away from a modeled river reach, we never generate synthetic or simulated river numbers.

Instead, our transparent Explainable AI Flood Risk Engine detects the absence of river data and mathematically reallocates the 25% river weight directly to 24h precipitation, storm burst intensity, and 3-day forecast rainfall.
