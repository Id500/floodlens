False Color (Bands 7-2-1) Science

In the MODIS 7-2-1 composite, shortwave infrared (Band 7), near-infrared (Band 2), and red (Band 1) are assigned to Red, Green, and Blue channels respectively.

  • Water & Flooding: Water absorbs infrared radiation strongly and appears very dark blue to deep black.
  • Vegetation: Chlorophyll reflects NIR radiation brilliantly, rendering land vegetation in vibrant neon green.
  • Sediment & Mud: Suspended particulates cause turbid floodwaters to exhibit light cyan or brownish tones.

Scientific Distinction & Cloud Cover

Important Disciplinary Integrity: Ordinary optical satellite reflectance alone DOES NOT confirm emergency flooding. During active typhoons or monsoons, optical satellites cannot see through dense cloud decks. Certified disaster management requires Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR / Sentinel-1) or validated hydrological sensors.

GIBS true and false color layers provide synoptic environmental context before and after cyclone passes.
